Tourism in Australia (2019–2024)

Explore how Australians travelled domestically and how international visitors spent their time and money across the country. This dashboard highlights key trends, surprising shifts, and interesting patterns in Australian tourism.

Domestic Trips in Australia (2019-2024)

Australians are increasingly exploring their own backyard. Notice how popular destinations like New South Wales and Queensland consistently attract the highest number of trips. The darker the region, the more travel it receives. Can you spot the shifts in domestic travel patterns over the years, especially after 2020?

International Arrivals (2019-2024)

International tourism saw dramatic changes in recent years. Observe where visitors enter the country and which locations are most popular. The size of the symbols indicates the number of arrivals. Look carefully at the dip in 2021, can you see how global events affected arrivals?

Domestic vs International Spending

Tracking spending helps us understand the economic impact of tourism. Domestic travellers usually spend more consistently across states, with a noticeable dip in 2020, likely due to COVID-19 travel restrictions. International visitor spending, however, shows a sharper decline in 2021, reflecting stricter border closures and travel limitations. Both domestic and international tourism show strong recovery in the subsequent years. Click on the legend to highlight individual states and explore trends over time.

Top 5 Visitor Countries Per Year

Australia’s international visitors come from a variety of countries, but some dominate the market each year. Observe which countries consistently contribute the most visitors and how this changes over time. Can you spot emerging markets or countries that rebounded quickly after 2021?
